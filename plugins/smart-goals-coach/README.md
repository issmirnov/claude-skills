# SMART Goals Coach

A guided session with Claude that turns a fuzzy wish ("get in shape", "save more") into one to three goals you can start this week. Made by Smirnov Labs.

## Use it

Tell Claude you want to set some goals, or ask it to run a SMART goals session. Claude asks one question at a time and walks you through the five checks:

- **Specific:** what exactly you'll do
- **Measurable:** the number or proof that tells you it's done
- **Achievable:** what time and energy you can give it, with a quick confidence check
- **Relevant:** why it matters to you now
- **Time-bound:** a deadline and check-in dates

At the end you get a goal card with your final goal, a first step for the next 48 hours, and an if-then plan for the most likely obstacle.

Two shortcuts also work:

- Paste a goal and ask Claude to make it SMART. It rewrites the goal and asks only for the missing pieces.
- Paste a list of goals and ask for a review. Claude scores each against the five checks and suggests one fix per goal.

## Data

This plugin contains instructions only. It has no connectors or scripts, sends nothing to any outside service, and stores nothing. Your conversation stays in your Claude account like any other chat.
