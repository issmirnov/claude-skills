# Question bank

Follow-up questions and common traps for each check. Use one follow-up at a time, picked for what the person just said. Don't read the list out.

## S: Specific

Opening: "What exactly will you do, or what will exist, when this is done?"

Follow-ups:
- "If I followed you around for a week, what would I see you doing differently?"
- "Which part of [their area] matters most? Pick one for now."
- "Is that the result you want, or the activity that gets you there? Either works; I just want to know which we're writing."

Traps:
- **Too broad.** "Be healthier", "grow the business". Ask them to pick the one piece that matters most this season.
- **Several goals in a trench coat.** "Get fit, sleep more and stop drinking soda." Split them and ask which comes first.
- **Stated as avoidance.** "Stop procrastinating." Ask what they'll do instead; positive actions are easier to track.

## M: Measurable

Opening: "How will you know it's done? What number or proof would convince you?"

Follow-ups:
- "Where are you today on that number?" (A target without a baseline hides how big the jump is.)
- "Is there something you can count every week, so you can see progress before the deadline?"
- "If it's hard to count, what would count as clear proof? A finished draft, a signed offer, a completed race?"

Traps:
- **Unmeasurable words.** More, better, often, regularly. Ask "how many?" or "how often?"
- **Only a lagging number.** "Lose 10 pounds" or "earn $10K" can take weeks to move. Pair it with a weekly behavior they control, such as workouts logged or outreach messages sent.

## A: Achievable

Opening: "What time, money and energy can you realistically give this each week?"

Then always ask: "On a scale of 1 to 10, how confident are you that you'll hit this?"

- **7 or higher:** move on.
- **Below 7:** ask "What would make it a 7?" and adjust. Options: smaller target, later date, fewer sessions per week, or removing a dependency on someone else.

Follow-ups:
- "Have you tried something like this before? What got in the way?"
- "What else is competing for that same time?"
- "Does this depend on anyone else saying yes? Can we rewrite it around the part you control?"

Traps:
- **Hero mode.** Daily habits from zero. Suggest starting at three times a week and earning daily.
- **Outcome they don't control.** "Get promoted." Rewrite around what they control: "Ship X and ask for a promotion review by June 1."

## R: Relevant

Opening: "Why does this matter to you right now?"

Follow-ups:
- "What changes in your life when this is done?"
- "Is this something you want, or something you feel you should want?" (Should-goals are the first to go.)
- "Does it pull against any of your other goals? Which wins if they clash?"

Traps:
- **Borrowed goal.** It's their partner's, boss's or the internet's goal. Ask what version of it they'd choose for themselves.
- **No reason given.** Don't force a profound answer. "I'd like to" is a fine reason; just get it in their words.

## T: Time-bound

Opening: "When do you want this done by?"

Follow-ups:
- "When's your first check-in? Something in the next 2 to 3 weeks works well."
- "Is there a natural deadline, like an event, a season or a trip, that we can anchor to?"
- "If it's ongoing, what's the first milestone date?"

Traps:
- **Someday.** "This year" or "eventually". Ask for a date.
- **Too far out.** Goals more than about 3 months away tend to drift. Suggest a first milestone within 6 to 12 weeks.
