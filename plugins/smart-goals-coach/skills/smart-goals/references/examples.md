# Before and after examples

Use these to show what a finished goal looks like, or to give someone a starting point when they're stuck. Adapt them to the person's own words, numbers and dates; never hand one over as their goal.

## Health

- **Before:** "Get in shape."
- **After:** "Run a 5K without stopping by March 1, training three mornings a week, so I have more energy for weekend hikes."

## Food (behavior, not body weight)

- **Before:** "Eat better."
- **After:** "Cook dinner at home at least four nights a week through the end of April, tracked on a note on the fridge, because I want to stop feeling sluggish after takeout."

## Career

- **Before:** "Get a better job."
- **After:** "Apply to 10 product-manager roles that pay at least my current salary by May 15, sending two applications a week and asking two former colleagues for referrals, because I want to move closer to customers."

## Money

- **Before:** "Save more."
- **After:** "Build a $3,000 emergency fund by September 30 by moving $250 into savings every payday, so a surprise car repair doesn't go on a credit card."

## Learning

- **Before:** "Learn Spanish."
- **After:** "Hold a 10-minute conversation in Spanish with my tutor by June 1, practicing 20 minutes a day on weekdays, so I can talk with my in-laws on our summer trip."

## Relationships

- **Before:** "See friends more."
- **After:** "Have one planned catch-up with a friend every week through the end of March, scheduled each Sunday night, because I've felt isolated since moving."

## Small team or business

- **Before:** "Improve customer support."
- **After:** "Cut median first-response time on support emails from 26 hours to under 8 hours by the end of Q2, by adding a shared inbox and a morning triage slot, because slow replies are our top complaint."

## Quick-fix example

Input: "I want to write a book this year."

Output:
> Rough SMART version: "Finish a first draft of **[about 60,000 words?]** by **[December 1?]**, writing **[500 words, 5 days a week?]**, because **[your reason]**."
>
> Three things to confirm: is 60,000 words about right for your book, does December 1 work, and why does this book matter to you now?
